// After the service //

Two printers, a garage, and a lot of stubbornness.

Drop Zone Print started the way most veteran businesses do: a problem nobody was solving, and somebody too stubborn to let it go. A broken gear clip. A coin display that didn’t exist. A part the manufacturer stopped making a decade ago.

So we printed it. Then we printed one for the guy next door, then one for his unit, and it kept going from there.

The name comes from the drop zone — the place where everything you packed either works or it doesn’t. Same standard here. Every part gets checked before it goes in the box, and if it isn’t right, it doesn’t ship.
